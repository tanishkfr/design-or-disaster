# Design or Disaster?

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
An interactive investigation into design perception and judgment. Visitors locate evidence, write a ruling, and compare five authored perspectives. The record describes attention, never correctness.

## Capabilities and Constraints
React/Vite, CSS Modules, ten existing cases, browser-local persistence. Preserve the five-minute path, movement progression, sealed finale, existing submissions and tied report insights. No services, accounts, invented cases or live expert claims.

## Brand Commitments
Phase 2.0 brief selects Dark Editorial Forensics, the D/D slash, dark main pages and signal-red accents. The signature experience is incompatible perspectives sharing one interface coordinate system.

## Evidence on Hand
src/data/cases.js and src/data/jurors.js provide authored content; public/screenshots contains the exhibits. SPEC.md records the v1 behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Pointer, touch, keyboard and named-region evidence input; visible focus, reduced motion and responsive layouts.

## Phase Boundary
Phase 2.0 supplies the visual foundations and Split, Instrument and Reveal. Phase 2.1 implements the full Second Look loop for The Benefits Form only, with an explicitly authored local reconstruction. The five-case collection, contradiction engine, cross-case analysis and export remain deferred to Phase 2.2.
